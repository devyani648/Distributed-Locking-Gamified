LOCKSTATE: QUORUM BREACH

Mission-oriented action prototype inspired by distributed database systems

Quick Tutorial

WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold click to shoot.

Press E near a tower to lock or unlock vote control.

Keep at least 3 towers locked and alive for 12 seconds.

Once consensus is stable, the boss shield drops.

Press H to hide/show this help. Press P to pause.

Controls: WASD move, mouse aim, click shoot, E lock/unlock tower, Shift dash, H help, P pause
